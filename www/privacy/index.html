Happy Endings

Privacy Policy

Effective August 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Late Night Studios (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you use Happy Endings, its website, online leaderboard, Community Wardrobe, Discord Rich Presence, and related services (collectively, the “Services”).

1. Information stored on your device

The game stores progress, settings, achievements, onboarding status, and similar gameplay information locally on your device using browser or application storage. This information normally stays on your device unless a feature described below sends specific data to an online service.

2. Analytics information

Our website and web-based game use Google Firebase Analytics. Depending on your device and settings, this may collect or derive information such as device and browser characteristics, general interaction events, session information, approximate location, and online identifiers. Google may process IP addresses and use cookies or similar technologies to provide the service. We use analytics to understand reliability, usage, and game performance.

3. Leaderboard information

If you use the leaderboard, Firebase Anonymous Authentication creates a random account identifier. The game may send that identifier and the following gameplay information to Google Firebase:

  • the parlor name you choose;
  • customers served and leaderboard rank inputs;
  • parlor tier and rebirth level;
  • lifetime in-game cash totals; and
  • record update timestamps.

The leaderboard publicly displays the chosen parlor name and selected gameplay statistics. Daily, weekly, and monthly customer totals use UTC reset periods, while the all-time board remains cumulative for the active leaderboard profile. Do not use your real name or other personal information as a parlor name.

4. Community Wardrobe information

If you publish or report an Outfit Workshop creation, the game uses the same Firebase Anonymous Authentication identifier described above. A published creation may include:

  • the parlor name shown as the creator name;
  • the creation's name, type, pixel-layer data, body-fit variants, palette, and the in-game body, face, hairstyle, and colors used for its original-model preview;
  • creation and update timestamps; and
  • the anonymous account identifier used to manage publishing rights.

Published creations and creator names are visible to other players. Do not put your real name or other personal information in a parlor or creation name. When you add a community creation, we store its identifier with your anonymous account identifier so the public wardrobe count increases only once per player. Creator names and aggregate wardrobe-add totals appear on the public outfit-creator leaderboard, and we may mark selected creations as featured. Reports include the creation identifier, a report reason, a timestamp, and the reporter's anonymous identifier; add records and reports are not publicly visible.

5. Discord Rich Presence

The desktop game can send your leaderboard rank and customers-served total to the Discord desktop client running on your device so Discord can display them on your profile. This feature does not give us access to your Discord password, private messages, or friends list. Discord processes Rich Presence information under its own privacy policy, and you can control activity sharing in Discord’s privacy settings.

6. Steam and other platforms

Steam builds may use Steamworks for achievements, ownership checks, and platform functionality. Valve and any other store or platform you use process account and transaction information under their own privacy policies. We do not receive your payment-card details.

7. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the Services, operate and moderate the leaderboard and Community Wardrobe, save and display game progress, diagnose failures, understand performance, prevent abuse, and improve the game.

8. Service providers and disclosure

We use service providers including Google Firebase and Google Analytics, Discord, and Valve/Steam. They process information as needed to provide their services. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect users or the Services, or as part of a business reorganization. We do not sell personal information.

9. Retention and security

Local game data remains until you clear it, reset the game, or uninstall the application, subject to platform backup behavior. Online leaderboard, Community Wardrobe, report, and analytics information is retained for as long as reasonably needed to operate, secure, moderate, and improve the Services or meet legal obligations. Unpublishing removes the public server copy, but local copies previously added by other players may remain on their devices. We use reasonable safeguards, but no storage or transmission system is completely secure.

10. Your choices and requests

  • You can clear local game data through the game or your device’s storage controls.
  • You can disable Discord activity sharing in Discord.
  • You can avoid submitting identifying information as a parlor name.
  • You can unpublish one of your creations from the Outfit Workshop.
  • You can request removal of a leaderboard record or ask a privacy question through our support channel below. We may need information that lets us identify the relevant anonymous record.

11. Adults only

The Services are intended only for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly offer the Services to or collect personal information from children.

12. International processing

Our providers may process information in countries other than your own. Those countries may have different data-protection laws. Providers apply their own legal transfer mechanisms and safeguards.

13. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy when the Services, providers, or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date when a revision is published.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Late Night Studios through the official community and support server.
